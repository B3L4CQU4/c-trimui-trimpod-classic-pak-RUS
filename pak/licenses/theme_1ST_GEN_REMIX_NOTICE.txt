1ST_GEN_REMIX theme
===================

Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Original Rockbox theme page:
  https://themes.rockbox.org/index.php?themeid=3958

The original theme headers describe its icons as based on artwork by Susan
Kare. TrimPod Classic adapted the theme for its player interface. TrimPod(RUS)
further modifies the layouts for a logical 512x384 display, language-aware UI
fonts, and the shared header, controls and status elements. These theme
modifications are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.
