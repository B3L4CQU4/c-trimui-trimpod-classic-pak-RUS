GNU Wget 1.24.5
================

TrimPod(RUS) includes an unmodified AArch64 GNU Wget executable used only to
retrieve synchronized lyrics from LRCLIB over HTTPS.

Upstream project and source for version 1.24.5:
  https://www.gnu.org/software/wget/
  https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/wget/wget-1.24.5.tar.gz

Bundled AArch64 executable SHA-256:
  8BE6464DD41F601612F4CA73397E8EC756B2AB6056D4571B5D5F0A860751F6D2

The exact prebuilt AArch64 executable was obtained from the following
distribution artifact.  NextUI Music Player is not the developer or upstream
source of GNU Wget:
